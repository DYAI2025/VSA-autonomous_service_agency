## 📊 Outreach-Metriken & KPIs

### Wöchentliche Ziele
- **Cold Emails**: 50-100
- **LinkedIn Messages**: 20-30
- **Gespräche gebucht**: 5-10
- **Kunden gewonnen**: 1-2

### Conversion-Rates
- **Email → Conversation**: 5-10%
- **Conversation → Customer**: 20-30%
- **LinkedIn → Connection**: 30-50%
- **Connection → Conversation**: 10-20%

### Tracking
- **Email Open Rate**: Ziel >20%
- **Reply Rate**: Ziel >5%
- **Meeting Book Rate**: Ziel >2%
- **Customer Acquisition Cost**: Ziel <€500

## 🎯 A/B Testing Strategy

### Test-Variablen
1. **Subject Lines**: Short vs. Descriptive
2. **Personalization**: Generic vs. Specific
3. **CTA**: Direct vs. Soft
4. **Timing**: Morning vs. Afternoon

### Test-Framework
- **Week 1-2**: Test Subject Lines
- **Week 3-4**: Test Personalization
- **Week 5-6**: Test CTA
- **Week 7+**: Optimize based on data
