# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) - Autonomous Pitch Deck Agency

**Agency**: Autonomous Pitch Deck Agency
**Target Market**: DACH Region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland)
**Focus**: B2B SaaS Startups
**Last updated**: 2026-05-18

---

## Executive Summary

Our ideal customers are Seed to Series A B2B SaaS startups in the DACH region who are preparing for institutional fundraising and need professional pitch deck services. These companies typically have strong technology/product but need help articulating their story to investors.

---

## Firmographics

### Company Stage
- **Primary**: Seed Stage (€1M-€5M raised, 10-30 employees)
- **Secondary**: Series A (€5M-€15M raised, 30-50 employees)
- **Revenue**: €100K-€5M ARR
- **Growth**: 2-3x year-over-year minimum

### Geography
- **Primary Markets**: 
  - Germany: Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt
  - Austria: Vienna, Graz
  - Switzerland: Zurich, Geneva
- **Language**: German-speaking markets with international ambitions
- **Expansion**: Planning or executing DACH-to-Europe expansion

### Sector Focus
**Primary Sectors**:
- B2B SaaS (highest priority)
- Enterprise Software
- Fintech
- Deep Tech
- Climate Tech
- Digital Health

**Secondary Sectors**:
- Marketplace platforms
- Developer tools
- Data infrastructure

**Avoid**:
- B2C consumer apps
- E-commerce (unless B2B focus)
- Hardware/physical products
- Crypto/Web3 (unless institutional focus)

### Company Characteristics
- **Product**: SaaS or software-enabled service
- **Business Model**: Subscription (MRR/ARR focus)
- **Team Size**: 10-50 employees
- **Funding History**: At least Seed round completed
- **Next Raise**: Planning €2M-€15M round in 3-12 months
- **Investor Base**: Mix of angels, seed funds, some institutional

### Technology Stack
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: AWS, Azure, GCP (modern stack)
- **Development**: Agile methodologies, modern frameworks
- **Product**: SaaS platform with clear value proposition
- **Data**: Some level of data-driven decision making

- **Product-Market Fit**: Some validation (paying customers, retention metrics)
- **Traction**: Measurable growth metrics (users, revenue, engagement)

- **Scalability**: Technology and business model designed for growth

## Behavioral Characteristics

### Pain Points We Solve
1. **Storytelling Gap**: Technical founders struggle with narrative
2. **Investor Communication**: Complex products hard to explain simply
3. **Market Positioning**: Unclear competitive differentiation
4. **Financial Story**: Metrics not presented compellingly
5. **Cross-Cultural**: Need to appeal to international investors

### Buying Signals
- **Active Fundraising**: Currently raising or planning next 6 months
- **Investor Meetings**: Already having conversations with VCs
- **Team Expansion**: Hiring for sales/marketing roles
- **Market Expansion**: Entering new geographies or segments
- **Competitive Pressure**: Competitors raising or succeeding

### Decision Making Process
- **Primary Decision Maker**: Founder/CEO (80% of cases)
- **Influencers**: COO, VP of Marketing, existing investors
- **Timeline**: 2-4 weeks from first contact to engagement
- **Budget**: €10K-€30K for pitch deck services
- **Decision Criteria**: Quality, speed, understanding of DACH market

## Psychographics

### Founder Profile
- **Background**: Technical (engineering, science) or business with technical domain
- **Experience**: First-time or second-time founders
- **Mindset**: Growth-oriented, ambitious, willing to invest in professional services
- **Pain**: Frustrated with current fundraising progress
- **Goal**: Secure institutional funding to scale

### Company Culture
- **Innovation-focused**: Embrace new approaches and best practices
- **Data-driven**: Value metrics and analytics
- **Professional**: Operate with business discipline
- **International**: Think beyond local market
- **Collaborative**: Willing to work closely with external partners

## Anti-Patterns (Who We Avoid)

### Not Ideal Customers
- **Too Early**: Pre-seed or idea-stage companies
- **Too Late**: Series B+ or already have strong in-house marketing
- **Wrong Sector**: B2C, hardware, crypto without institutional focus
- **Wrong Mindset**: Not willing to invest in professional services
- **Geographic**: Outside DACH region (unless special case)
- **Revenue Model**: Not subscription/SaaS-based

### Red Flags
- No clear funding timeline
- No existing product or customers
- Unwilling to share information or collaborate
- Expecting unrealistic results
- Budget misalignment
